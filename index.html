About Me

I am Jianxiong Fan, a researcher interested in human–computer interaction, cognition, and interaction design. I hold a Master's degree in Design Studies, specialising in Digital Creative Design Research, and a Bachelor's degree in Digital Media Technology from Guangdong University of Technology (GDUT).

My research examines cognitive and emotional dynamics in human–computer interaction through mixed methods. I combine quantitative user experience measurement and statistical analysis with qualitative inquiry to inform the design of products and service systems.

My recent work explores short-video entertainment-based learning for vocational training, with a focus on middle-aged and older adults with low educational attainment. I also study the cognitive, emotional, and behavioural mechanisms of interaction-based creative thinking. I am particularly interested in extending this work to AI-supported learning and human–AI collaboration.

Research & Technical Skills

  • Immersive interaction & creativity
