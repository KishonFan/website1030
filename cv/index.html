Research Experience

Short-Video Entertainment-Based Learning Design

Feb 2022 – Present

Design and Development of a Defensive Driving Training System · Project No. 23HS0022

  • Investigated learning needs and cognitive, emotional, and behavioural responses among hazardous chemical transport drivers.
  • Used interviews, factor analysis, and K-means clustering to characterise learners and inform an entertainment-based learning approach.
  • Led the design of a short-video training app, backend management system, and immersive VR assessment platform.
  • Conducted a BIOPAC-based experiment with 48 target-group drivers and 48 younger university participants; compared physiological responses and preferences across short-video and text-and-graphics materials.
  • Analysed survey data from a six-month programme (N = 333) using an integrated TPB–TAM model (R² = .516).
  • Under academic supervision, proposed a three-dimensional cognitive-load framework. Outputs include two first-author journal articles, one manuscript under review, and one in preparation.

Mechanisms of Interaction-Based Creative Thinking

Sep 2023 – Present

Guangdong Basic and Applied Basic Research Foundation · Grant 2026A1515012303

  • Studied cognitive, emotional, and behavioural processes in artistic collage, comparing interaction-based and divergence-based thinking.
  • Contributed to literature review, experimental design, think-aloud protocols, cognitive and behavioural coding, and lag sequential analysis.
  • Co-authored two published journal articles and contributed to a submitted manuscript.
  • Led a follow-up EEG and eye-tracking study on emotional text; designed materials and procedures, established multimodal data collection protocols, and analysed EEG and eye-tracking data.
